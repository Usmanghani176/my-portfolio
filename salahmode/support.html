Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the prayer times?

+
Salah Mode uses established Islamic calculation methods (Muslim World League, ISNA, etc.) and your device's GPS location to calculate prayer times. While we strive for accuracy, we recommend verifying with your local mosque for the most precise times, especially during critical periods like Ramadan.

Does Salah Mode work offline?

+
Yes! Salah Mode is designed to work completely offline. Once you've set your location, all prayer times, Qibla direction, and features work without an internet connection. Your data is stored locally on your device.

What is Salah Mode and how does it work?

+
Salah Mode is a focus feature that helps you maintain concentration during prayer by minimizing distractions. When activated, it can silence notifications and provide a dedicated prayer environment. You can customize its behavior in the app settings.

How do I calculate Zakat using the app?

+
Navigate to the Zakat section and enter your assets (cash, gold, silver, investments, etc.). The app will automatically calculate whether you've reached the nisab threshold and how much Zakat is due. Remember to consult with a scholar for complex financial situations.

Can I change the prayer calculation method?

+
Yes! Go to Settings and select your preferred calculation method. We support multiple methods including Muslim World League, ISNA, Umm al-Qura, and others. Choose the method that aligns with your local mosque or Islamic authority.

Is my data synchronized to any server?

+
No. Salah Mode uses an "offline-first" approach. All your prayer records, settings, and personal data are stored exclusively on your device. We do not maintain central servers for user data, ensuring maximum privacy.
