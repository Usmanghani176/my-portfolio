Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Salah Mode ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to helping Muslims maintain their prayer schedule and strengthen their connection with Allah. We prioritize your privacy above all else. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data within the Salah Mode application.

2. "Offline-First" Privacy

Salah Mode is designed as an "offline-first" application. This means that, by default, all user data—including your prayer times, Qibla direction preferences, Zakat calculations, fasting records, and app settings—is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain any central servers that store your personal religious data.

3. Data We Collect

To provide our services, we may collect the following information locally on your device:

  • Location Data: Your location is used solely to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction. This data is processed locally and never transmitted to external servers.
  • Prayer Records: Logs of your prayer completion, Salah Mode activation, and personal prayer statistics.
  • App Preferences: Notification settings, calculation methods, and customization preferences.
  • Zakat & Fasting Data: Financial calculations for Zakat and fasting records you choose to track.

4. No Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Because your data remains on your device, we do not have access to it, and therefore cannot provide it to advertisers or other external entities.

5. Third-Party Services

The app may use minimal third-party integrations to enhance user experience:

  • Prayer Time Calculations: We use established Islamic calculation methods (e.g., Muslim World League, ISNA) processed locally on your device.
  • Local Notifications: To send prayer reminders and Salah Mode alerts (requires user permission).
  • Google Fonts: To provide a consistent visual interface.

These services do not receive your personal prayer logs or location data.

6. Your Rights & Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete all your information at any time using the app's settings or by uninstalling the application. This action is irreversible as we do not keep backups of your local data.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app and updating the "Last Updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about our privacy practices, please contact us through the official support channels provided in the app or via our support page.
