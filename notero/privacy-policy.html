Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Notero ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to helping users organize their thoughts and capture ideas effortlessly. We prioritize your privacy above all else. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data within the Notero application.

2. "Offline-First" Privacy

Notero is designed as an "offline-first" application. This means that, by default, all user data—including your notes, categories, and voice recordings—is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain any central servers that store your personal note data.

3. Data We Collect

To provide our services, we may collect the following information locally on your device:

  • Personal Information: Minimal profile info if synced with external services (optional).
  • Note Data: Content of your notes, categories, tags, and timestamps.
  • Usage Data: Application usage statistics to improve user experience.

4. No Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Because your data remains on your device, we do not have access to it, and therefore cannot provide it to advertisers or other external entities.

5. Third-Party Services

The app may use minimal third-party integrations to enhance user experience:

  • Google Fonts: To provide a consistent visual interface.
  • Local Notifications: To send reminders (requires user permission).

These services do not receive your personal notes or information.

6. Your Rights & Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete all your information at any time using the "Factory Reset" or "Clear All Data" options in the system settings. This action is irreversible as we do not keep backups of your local data.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app and updating the "Last Updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about our privacy practices, please contact us through the official support channels provided in the app description.
