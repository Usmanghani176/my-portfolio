Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data synchronized to any server?

+
Notero uses an "offline-first" approach. All your notes, categories, and logs are stored exclusively on your device. We do not maintain central servers for user note data, ensuring maximum privacy.

What happens if I lose my device?

+
Since data is stored locally, it cannot be recovered from a new device unless you have a high-level system backup (like iCloud or Google Drive) that includes app data.

How can I reset all my progress?

+
You can perform a "Factory Reset" within the App Settings. Please note that this action is irreversible and will permanently delete all your notes and profile data.
